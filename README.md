# Your Step is Energy | خطوتك طاقة

An innovative sustainable energy project that explores how pedestrian movement can be converted into small amounts of usable electrical energy through a smart floor tile.

The project was awarded **1st Place at the college level in the APEX Exhibition**.

## Project Overview

Your Step is Energy is a prototype smart tile designed to benefit from the kinetic energy generated by footsteps.

When pressure is applied to the tile, piezoelectric components generate electrical voltage. The generated energy is then managed, stored, and displayed through a simple monitoring interface.

The current version is an initial prototype intended to demonstrate the concept and measure the generated energy.

## Problem

Public spaces such as walkways, schools, universities, parks, and high-traffic facilities experience continuous pedestrian movement.

The project explores how part of this movement can be used to support awareness of clean energy and small-scale energy harvesting applications.

## Proposed Solution

The proposed solution is a smart tile that:

- Captures energy from repeated footsteps
- Converts mechanical pressure into electrical energy
- Stores the generated energy
- Displays the generated output
- Provides a practical demonstration of sustainable energy harvesting

## How It Works

The prototype follows this basic process:

1. Footstep pressure
2. Voltage generation using piezoelectric discs
3. Energy management using the LTC3588
4. Energy storage
5. Display and monitoring

## Main Components

- Piezoelectric discs
- LTC3588 energy harvesting component
- Capacitor / energy storage component
- 3.7V battery
- Charging and protection circuit
- Voltage converter
- Display / monitoring interface
